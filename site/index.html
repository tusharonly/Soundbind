Needs Homebrew. Works on macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

Or download the disk image
Downloaded it and macOS will not open it?

Soundbind is not notarized by Apple yet, so a downloaded copy is blocked with “Apple could not verify Soundbind”. Click Done, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Installing with Homebrew avoids this. Or run this once in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Soundbind.app
